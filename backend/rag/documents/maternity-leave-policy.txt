EnterpriseCorp Maternity Leave Policy
EnterpriseCorp provides 26 weeks of fully paid maternity leave to female employees welcoming a child through birth or adoption. To be eligible for the full 26 weeks of paid leave, the employee must have completed a minimum of 12 consecutive months of continuous employment at EnterpriseCorp prior to the expected date of delivery or adoption placement. Employees with less than 12 months of tenure are eligible for 12 weeks of paid leave and an additional 14 weeks of unpaid leave. Expectant mothers may commence their maternity leave up to 8 weeks prior to their expected delivery date. Employees must notify the HR department and their direct manager of their intention to take maternity leave at least 15 weeks prior to the expected start date, accompanied by a medical certificate or relevant adoption documentation. During the paid maternity leave period, employees will continue to receive their standard base salary, and all company benefits, including health insurance and provident fund contributions, will remain active. Following the conclusion of the 26-week leave period, employees have the option to request an additional 12 weeks of unpaid extended leave, subject to managerial approval based on operational requirements. Returning mothers are entitled to flexible working hours, including a gradual return-to-work schedule consisting of a 4-day work week for the first month at full pay.
