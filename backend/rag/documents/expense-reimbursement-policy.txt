EnterpriseCorp Expense Reimbursement Policy
EnterpriseCorp reimburses employees for reasonable and necessary expenses incurred directly in the course of approved business activities. Eligible expenses include client meals, local transit to client sites, and approved professional development materials. All expenses must be submitted through the company financial portal within 30 days of the transaction date. Reimbursement requests must include a high-resolution digital scan or photograph of the original itemized receipt; credit card summary statements are not accepted as valid proof of purchase. Client meals are capped at $75 per person, including tax and a maximum 20 percent gratuity. The names of all attendees and the specific business purpose of the meal must be documented in the submission. Expenditures exceeding $200 require documented pre-approval from the employee direct line manager prior to the purchase. The Finance department processes approved reimbursements bi-weekly, issuing payments via direct deposit to the employee designated payroll account. Expenses submitted after the 30-day window will be rejected unless accompanied by an exception waiver signed by the Department Vice President. The purchase of alcohol is strictly non-reimbursable unless explicitly pre-authorized for specific corporate-sponsored events.
