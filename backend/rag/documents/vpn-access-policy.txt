EnterpriseCorp VPN Access Policy
The EnterpriseCorp Virtual Private Network allows authorized remote users to securely access the internal corporate network and private servers. All remote work must be conducted through the official VPN client when accessing internal databases, the intranet, or staging environments. VPN access is provisioned automatically for full-time employees upon completion of IT onboarding. Contractors and temporary staff must submit a formal access request, approved by a Department Director, specifying the exact duration and scope of required access. The VPN client enforces a strict idle timeout; connections will be automatically severed after 30 minutes of inactivity, requiring the user to re-authenticate using Multi-Factor Authentication. Split-tunneling is disabled by default, ensuring all remote traffic is routed through the company central security firewalls and content filters. Employees must not share their VPN credentials or allow non-employees to utilize their active connection. Connecting to the VPN via unsecured, public Wi-Fi networks found in coffee shops or airports is prohibited unless the employee is concurrently utilizing the IT-approved secondary encryption wrapper. Any detected anomalous login locations or simultaneous geographic logins will result in immediate automated suspension of the user VPN privileges pending IT review.
