EnterpriseCorp Paternity Leave Policy
EnterpriseCorp grants 4 weeks of fully paid paternity leave to eligible male employees following the birth or adoption of a child. This policy applies to all full-time employees who are the biological father or the legally recognized adoptive parent of the child, provided they have completed a minimum of 6 months of continuous service with the company. Employees with less than 6 months of service are entitled to 2 weeks of paid paternity leave. Paternity leave must be taken within the first 6 months following the child birth or adoption placement. The leave can be taken consecutively as a 4-week block or split into two separate 2-week blocks. Employees must formally apply for paternity leave through the HR portal at least 8 weeks prior to the expected delivery or adoption date, acknowledging that actual dates may shift. In instances of premature birth or medical emergencies, the advance notice requirement is waived, but employees must notify HR within 48 hours of the event. During paternity leave, the employee base salary and all benefits, including health coverage and pension accrual, remain fully intact. Unused paternity leave cannot be cashed out, transferred to another individual, or carried over beyond the 6-month eligibility window.
