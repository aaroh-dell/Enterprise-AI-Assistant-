EnterpriseCorp Learning and Development Stipend Policy
EnterpriseCorp actively encourages continuous professional growth by providing an annual Learning and Development stipend of $1,500 to all full-time employees who have completed at least 6 months of continuous service. The stipend resets annually on January 1st and does not carry over to subsequent years. Eligible expenses include industry conferences, professional certification exams, online course subscriptions, and job-related academic textbooks. To utilize the stipend, employees must submit a proposal to their direct manager detailing the cost of the resource and its direct relevance to their current role or established career progression plan. Upon managerial approval, the employee may either request the company to pay the vendor directly via corporate card or purchase the resource personally and submit for reimbursement. If an employee resigns or is terminated for cause within 6 months of the company funding a certification or conference exceeding $500, the employee must repay 50 percent of the total cost, which will be withheld from their final paycheck. Mandatory compliance training mandated by the company does not count against the employee annual balance.
