EnterpriseCorp Retirement and Provident Fund Policy
To assist employees in building long-term financial security, EnterpriseCorp offers a robust retirement savings program. Full-time employees are automatically enrolled in the corporate 401k or regional Provident Fund equivalent upon the completion of their 90-day probationary period. The default employee contribution rate is set at 5 percent of the employee pre-tax base salary, though employees may manually adjust this percentage or opt out entirely via the HR benefits portal. EnterpriseCorp provides a generous employer matching program, matching 100 percent of the first 4 percent of the employee contribution, and 50 percent of the next 2 percent, resulting in a maximum company match of 5 percent of the base salary. Employer matching contributions are subject to a 3-year graded vesting schedule. Employees become 33 percent vested in the company contributions after 1 year of continuous service, 66 percent vested after 2 years, and fully vested at 100 percent upon completing 3 years of service. Employee personal contributions are always 100 percent immediately vested. Administrative fees for the retirement accounts are fully subsidized by the company.
