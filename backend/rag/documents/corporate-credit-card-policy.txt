EnterpriseCorp Corporate Credit Card Policy
EnterpriseCorp issues corporate credit cards to employees whose roles require frequent travel, client entertainment, or regular departmental purchasing. Corporate cards are to be used exclusively for authorized business expenditures; personal use of the corporate card is strictly prohibited, regardless of intention to reimburse the company. The standard monthly credit limit is set at $3,000 for individual contributors and $10,000 for department heads. Cardholders are entirely responsible for the security of their physical card and must immediately report lost or stolen cards to the issuing bank and the Finance department. By the 5th day of each month, cardholders must log into the expense management system to reconcile their previous month statement, allocating each transaction to the correct general ledger code and attaching itemized receipts for all transactions exceeding $25. Failure to reconcile the statement by the 10th day of the month will result in a temporary suspension of the card. A second consecutive failure to reconcile on time will result in permanent revocation of the corporate card and potential formal disciplinary action regarding financial negligence.
