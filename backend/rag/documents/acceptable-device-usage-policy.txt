EnterpriseCorp Acceptable Device Usage Policy
EnterpriseCorp provides employees with company-owned laptops, mobile devices, and peripherals necessary to fulfill their job responsibilities. These devices remain the exclusive property of the company and are intended primarily for business use. Incidental personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with productivity, consume excessive network bandwidth, or violate the Code of Conduct. Employees are strictly prohibited from using company devices to access, download, or distribute illegal content, unauthorized peer-to-peer file-sharing platforms, or online gambling services. Users must not attempt to bypass local administrative restrictions, disable pre-installed security software, or alter endpoint management configurations. All devices are monitored for security and compliance purposes; employees should have no expectation of privacy regarding any data, communications, or browsing history generated on company equipment. In the event a device is lost, stolen, or damaged, the employee must report the incident to the IT Service Desk within 4 hours to initiate remote data wipe protocols. Upon termination of employment, all hardware must be returned to the IT department within 3 business days. Failure to return equipment will result in the depreciated cost of the hardware being deducted from the employee final salary settlement.
