EnterpriseCorp Corporate Travel Budget Policy
This policy outlines the budgeting and booking guidelines for all domestic and international business travel. All travel must be booked exclusively through the EnterpriseCorp designated travel management platform. Flights must be booked at least 14 days in advance to secure optimal pricing. Economy class is mandatory for all domestic flights and international flights with a total duration of under 8 hours. Business class travel is permitted only for continuous international flights exceeding 8 hours and requires approval from the Chief Financial Officer. The maximum nightly hotel allowance is $150 for domestic travel and $250 for designated high-cost international metropolitan areas. Employees receive a fixed daily per diem for meals and incidental expenses of $65 for domestic travel and $90 for international travel. Per diems are paid in advance and do not require itemized receipt submission, but are prorated at 75 percent for the designated days of departure and return. Car rentals are permitted only when cost-effective compared to local taxis or ride-share services, and employees must decline rental agency insurance as coverage is provided under the master corporate liability policy.
