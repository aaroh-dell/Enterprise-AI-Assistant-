EnterpriseCorp Annual Leave Policy
All full-time employees at EnterpriseCorp are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued at a rate of 1.66 days per month. Part-time employees accrue annual leave on a pro-rata basis corresponding to their contracted hours. Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days into the following calendar year. Any additional unused days beyond this limit will be forfeited on December 31st. Employees must submit leave requests through the HR portal at least 14 days in advance for absences exceeding 3 consecutive business days. For requests of 1 to 3 days, a minimum of 48 hours notice is required. Line managers are required to review and respond to all leave requests within 3 business days of submission. In the event of conflicting leave requests within the same department, priority will be granted based on the date of submission and critical project deadlines. Leave requests during the company designated blackout period, which occurs annually between December 15th and December 31st, require exceptional approval from the Department Director. Upon voluntary or involuntary termination of employment, employees will be compensated for accrued but unused annual leave days in their final paycheck at their standard daily base rate.
