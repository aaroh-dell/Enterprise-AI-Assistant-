EnterpriseCorp Data Security and Classification Policy
To protect corporate and client information, EnterpriseCorp mandates the strict classification and handling of all digital and physical data. Data is categorized into three tiers: Public, Internal, and Restricted. Public data is approved for external distribution. Internal data includes standard operational communications and unreleased marketing materials, which may not be shared outside the company without managerial consent. Restricted data encompasses personally identifiable information, financial records, client databases, and proprietary source code. Access to Restricted data is granted strictly on a role-based, least-privilege basis. Employees handling Restricted data must store files exclusively on the secure corporate server drives or the approved encrypted cloud storage environment. Transferring Restricted data to personal external hard drives, unauthorized USB flash drives, or personal email accounts is strictly prohibited and will trigger automated IT alerts. All email transmissions containing Restricted data to approved external partners must utilize the company secure email encryption tool. Employees are required to lock their computer screens when leaving their desks and must properly shred all physical documents containing Internal or Restricted data using the designated secure disposal bins located in the printing areas.
