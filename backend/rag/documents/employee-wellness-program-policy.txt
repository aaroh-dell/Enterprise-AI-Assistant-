EnterpriseCorp Employee Wellness Program Policy
EnterpriseCorp is committed to supporting the physical and mental well-being of its workforce through the Employee Wellness Program. All full-time and part-time employees are eligible for a monthly wellness stipend of $75. This stipend is added to the first paycheck of each month and is intended to cover expenses related to physical fitness, including gym memberships, yoga classes, fitness applications, or the purchase of home exercise equipment. No receipts are required to claim this monthly stipend. Additionally, the company provides a confidential Employee Assistance Program available 24 hours a day. The program offers up to 6 free, fully subsidized counseling sessions per calendar year per employee or immediate family member, addressing stress, anxiety, financial planning, or legal concerns. EnterpriseCorp also designates the second Friday of every quarter as a Recharge Day, which is a fully paid company-wide holiday designed to prevent burnout. Employees are strictly discouraged from sending internal emails or scheduling meetings on Recharge Days to ensure uninterrupted rest for all staff members.
