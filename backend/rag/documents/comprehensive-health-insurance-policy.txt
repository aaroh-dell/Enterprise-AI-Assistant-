EnterpriseCorp Comprehensive Health Insurance Policy
EnterpriseCorp provides comprehensive medical, dental, and vision insurance to all full-time employees and their eligible dependents, effective from the first day of the month following 30 days of continuous employment. The company subsidizes 85 percent of the monthly premium cost for the employee and 70 percent of the premium cost for a spouse, domestic partner, and dependent children under the age of 26. Employees may choose between a Preferred Provider Organization plan or a High Deductible Health Plan paired with a Health Savings Account. For employees opting into the High Deductible plan, the company provides an annual seed contribution of $1,000 for individuals and $2,000 for family coverage, deposited into the HSA in bi-annual installments in January and July. Open enrollment occurs annually during the month of November, during which employees may add or remove dependents or switch plan types. Outside of open enrollment, changes to coverage can only be made within 30 days of a qualifying life event, such as marriage, divorce, or the birth of a child, requiring the submission of legal documentation to the HR benefits coordinator.
