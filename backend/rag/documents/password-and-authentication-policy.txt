EnterpriseCorp Password and Authentication Policy
All employees must adhere to strict password protocols to safeguard EnterpriseCorp networks, applications, and sensitive data. User account passwords must contain a minimum of 14 characters and include at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one numerical digit, and one special character. Passwords must not contain the user name, employee ID, company name, or common dictionary words. The IT department enforces mandatory password expiration every 90 days. Upon expiration, employees cannot reuse any of their previous 5 passwords. Multi-Factor Authentication is deployed universally across all internal and cloud-based systems; employees are required to verify their identity using the company-approved authenticator application installed on their registered mobile device. Physical security keys are issued to system administrators and employees handling Tier 1 restricted data. Account lockouts will automatically occur after 5 consecutive failed login attempts within a 15-minute window. Locked accounts require a manual reset via the IT Service Desk and identity verification using an employee badge or verified alternate email. Writing down passwords on physical notes or storing them in unencrypted digital files is strictly prohibited and constitutes a severe security violation subject to formal disciplinary proceedings.
