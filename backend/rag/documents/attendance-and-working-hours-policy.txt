EnterpriseCorp Attendance and Working Hours Policy
The standard work week at EnterpriseCorp consists of 40 hours, scheduled from Monday to Friday. Standard office hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, which includes a mandatory 1-hour unpaid lunch break to be taken between 12:00 PM and 2:00 PM. Employees are expected to arrive on time and be prepared to commence work at their scheduled start time. Tardiness of more than 15 minutes must be reported to the direct manager via the internal communication platform prior to the start of the shift. An employee who accumulates three undocumented late arrivals within a single 30-day period will receive a formal verbal warning. Unplanned absences due to illness or personal emergencies must be communicated to the direct manager and HR no later than 9:30 AM on the day of the absence. Failure to report an absence for three consecutive business days will be considered job abandonment and may result in immediate termination of employment. Non-exempt employees who are requested by their manager to work beyond 40 hours in a single work week will be compensated at a rate of 1.5 times their regular hourly wage for the overtime hours, requiring prior written approval from the department head.
