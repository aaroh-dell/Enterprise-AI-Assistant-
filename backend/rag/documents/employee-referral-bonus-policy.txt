EnterpriseCorp Employee Referral Bonus Policy
EnterpriseCorp believes our employees are the best source for identifying top-tier talent. The Employee Referral Bonus Policy rewards current staff who successfully refer candidates for open full-time positions. The standard referral bonus is $1,500 for entry-level to mid-level roles, and $3,000 for senior management or specialized engineering positions, as clearly designated on the internal job board. To be eligible, the referring employee must submit the candidate resume through the applicant tracking system referral portal before the candidate applies independently. Vice Presidents, Human Resources staff, and the hiring manager directly responsible for the open role are ineligible to receive the referral bonus. The bonus payment is structured in two equal installments: 50 percent is paid on the referred candidate first day of employment, and the remaining 50 percent is paid upon the referred candidate successful completion of their 90-day probationary period. Both the referring employee and the referred candidate must be actively employed and in good standing with EnterpriseCorp on the scheduled payout dates for the bonus to be disbursed via the standard payroll cycle.
