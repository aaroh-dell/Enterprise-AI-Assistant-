EnterpriseCorp Procurement and Vendor Approval Policy
The procurement policy ensures that all goods and services purchased by EnterpriseCorp represent optimal value, quality, and compliance. Any departmental purchase under $1,000 requires only the approval of the direct Line Manager. Purchases ranging from $1,001 to $10,000 require the approval of the Department Director and the submission of at least two competitive vendor quotes. Capital expenditures or service contracts exceeding $10,000 mandate a formal Request for Proposal process, requiring a minimum of three competitive bids, followed by approval from the Chief Financial Officer and the Procurement Committee. Before engaging a new vendor, the vendor must pass a due diligence review covering financial stability, cybersecurity compliance, and legal standing, a process taking up to 10 business days. Employees are strictly forbidden from signing vendor contracts, master service agreements, or legally binding statements of work; all external contracts must be reviewed by the Legal department and signed exclusively by an authorized corporate officer. Department managers must formally review recurring vendor contracts annually 60 days prior to renewal dates to assess ongoing value and performance metrics.
