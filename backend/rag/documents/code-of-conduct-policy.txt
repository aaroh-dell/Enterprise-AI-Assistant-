EnterpriseCorp Code of Conduct Policy
The EnterpriseCorp Code of Conduct establishes the baseline expectations for professional behavior for all employees, contractors, and temporary staff. Employees must treat colleagues, clients, and vendors with respect, strictly prohibiting any form of discrimination, harassment, or bullying based on race, gender, age, religion, sexual orientation, or disability. Any observed or experienced violations must be reported immediately to the HR department or through the anonymous internal whistleblower hotline. Employees must avoid conflicts of interest, defined as situations where personal, financial, or familial relationships could improperly influence business decisions. Any potential conflict of interest, including secondary employment or consulting work, must be disclosed in writing to the Compliance Officer within 5 business days of the conflict arising. The acceptance of gifts, entertainment, or hospitality from clients or vendors is limited to items with a verifiable market value of less than $100 per calendar year from a single source. Furthermore, employees must protect the company intellectual property, utilizing confidential information solely for authorized business purposes. Violations of this Code of Conduct will result in disciplinary action, which may progress from formal written warnings to immediate termination of employment, depending on the severity and nature of the infraction.
