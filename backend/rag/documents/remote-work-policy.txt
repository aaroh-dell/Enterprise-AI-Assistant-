EnterpriseCorp Remote Work Policy
EnterpriseCorp operates on a hybrid work model, allowing eligible employees to work remotely for up to 3 days per calendar week. To qualify for remote work, employees must have successfully completed their 90-day probationary period and maintain a performance rating of Meets Expectations or higher. Remote work days must be scheduled in agreement with the employee direct manager at the beginning of each quarter to ensure adequate in-office coverage across all departments. Core collaboration days are designated as Tuesdays and Thursdays; all employees are required to be physically present in the office on these days unless on approved leave or business travel. While working remotely, employees must be online and accessible via company communication channels during the core operational hours of 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM local time. Employees are responsible for securing a reliable internet connection with a minimum download speed of 50 Mbps and a private, distraction-free workspace. EnterpriseCorp provides a one-time home office setup stipend of $500 for the purchase of ergonomic furniture or approved peripherals. The company reserves the right to suspend or revoke remote work privileges if an employee productivity declines, if communication protocols are ignored, or if project requirements demand full-time on-site presence.
